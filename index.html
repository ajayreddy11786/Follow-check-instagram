Who doesn't follow you back?

Your data

Drop your files here or tap to choose followers_1.json, following.json (or the .html versions)

How to get these files
  1. In Instagram, open Settings, then Accounts Center.
  2. Choose Your information and permissions, then Export your information.
  3. Create an export, pick Export to device, and select Followers and following.
  4. Set the format to JSON and the date range to All time, then start the export.
  5. When Instagram emails you the download, unzip it. The files are in the connections, followers_and_following folder.

Instagram does not let a website read follower lists from a username alone, so this uses your own download. Everything is processed in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
